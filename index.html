About the Portal

The Farmer Marketplace and Advisory Portal is a simple online platform that helps farmers list their produce, buy farm products, get practical agricultural guidance and track every order from placement to delivery. It brings buyers and sellers together without middlemen and puts useful farming knowledge one click away.

🌱 Agricultural Advisory

Practical guidance to help you grow healthier crops and better income.

🌾

Crop Selection

  • Choose crops that suit your local climate, rainfall and soil type.
  • Check market demand and price trends before planting.
  • Use certified, disease-free and high-yield seed varieties.
  • Rotate cereals with pulses to keep soil fertile.
🪱

Soil Preparation

  • Test your soil every 2-3 years for pH and nutrients.
  • Plough deeply after harvest and let the field sun-dry.
  • Mix well-rotted farmyard manure or compost before sowing.
  • Level the field for even water distribution.
💧

Irrigation Methods

  • Drip irrigation saves up to 50% water and suits vegetables and orchards.
  • Sprinklers work well for pulses, cereals and uneven land.
  • Water early morning or evening to reduce evaporation.
  • Avoid over-watering – it causes root rot and nutrient loss.
🧪

Fertilizers

  • Apply fertilizers based on soil-test recommendations.
  • Balance nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P) and potassium (K).
  • Combine organic manure with chemical fertilizers for lasting fertility.
  • Split nitrogen doses across crop stages to reduce wastage.
🐛

Pest Management

  • Inspect fields weekly to spot pests and diseases early.
  • Use neem-based sprays, pheromone and sticky traps first.
  • Encourage helpful insects such as ladybirds and spiders.
  • Use chemical pesticides only when needed, in the right dose.
📅

Seasonal Farming Tips

  • Kharif (Jun-Oct): rice, maize, cotton, groundnut – ensure good drainage.
  • Rabi (Nov-Mar): wheat, mustard, gram – plan irrigation carefully.
  • Zaid (Mar-Jun): watermelon, cucumber, fodder – protect from heat.
  • Follow weather forecasts before sowing and harvesting.
